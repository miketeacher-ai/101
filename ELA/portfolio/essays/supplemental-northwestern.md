Second, Kellogg's undergraduate access means I could pair pure economics with finance practice rather than choosing — my Money Skills workshop taught me teaching compound interest lands harder when you can also show loan contract. Third, quarter system rewards pace I trained at: my 45-day sprint built research paper, workshop series, and eight essays simultaneously, and I want more quarters like that.

Outside class, I'd bring workshop with me — financial literacy curriculum tested on thirty students in Ankara, ready to adapt to Evanston community partners. And I'd arrive knowing how to read data honestly: first charts ugly, first conclusions wrong, fixing them taught more than being right.

Northwestern is where I want to turn chalkboard habit into discipline.
