# Project 02: Financial Literacy Leadership (Days 16-30) — How to Build Each Day

**Student:** Ela | **Major:** Economics & Finance | **Sprint:** Day 1 Mon Aug 24 - Day 45 Wed Oct 7, 2026

Deliverable: "Money Skills / Para Okuryazarlığı" — 5-session workshop for 30+ students, 10 public posts, measured pre/post lift. 4-5 hrs/day. Optimized: pilot early, reusable curriculum, metrics over vanity.

## Curriculum: 5 Sessions × 60-90 min (optimized)

| # | Session | Content | Activity | How to Teach | Suggestion |
|---|---------|---------|----------|--------------|------------|
| 1 | Where money goes | Needs vs wants, tracking | Expense audit worksheet | Start with personal audit, not lecture — students fill their own spending first | First 5 min = doing, not talking — worksheet in hands immediately |
| 2 | Inflation and your pocket | Real vs nominal, her research data live | Price-basket game | Show her research chart live, then game: guess basket cost 2021 vs 2024 | Use her own chart — students see research is not abstract |
| 3 | Saving and goals | Emergency fund, compound growth demo (Colab) | Savings plan drafting | Live Colab demo: 100 lira/month at 10% for 10 years vs 5% — show compounding visually | Live demo beats slides — one interactive chart > five static ones |
| 4 | Debt literacy | Interest, credit traps, installment culture | Case-study debate | Present 2 case studies (installment phone vs saving), debate in pairs | Case studies with Turkish installment culture — relevant, not generic US examples |
| 5 | Earning basics | First income, simple budgeting | Mock invoice | Each student writes mock invoice for freelance work (tutoring, design) | Mock invoice makes earning tangible — they leave with something they made |

## Day-by-Day: How to Build + Suggestion

| Day | Task | How to Build | Suggestion / Optimization |
|-----|------|--------------|---------------------------|
| 16 | Secure venue + permission; pick dates | How: Write 1-page request (what, why, when, supervision, safety). Deliver in person with one-page curriculum overview. Offer to share impact report with school. | Suggestion: Offer impact report to administration — they get PR, you get venue. Ask janitor too — knows where outlets/projector are |
| 17 | Write session 1 deck + worksheet; recruit via poster + announcements | How: Deck: 10 slides max (title, 3 concepts, 1 activity, summary). Worksheet: expense audit (categories: food, transport, fun, savings). Poster: hand-drawn A3 with date + 3 benefits. Announce in 3 classes, 60 sec each. | Suggestion: Hand-drawn poster signals "we make things" better than Canva. 60-sec pitch > Instagram post for 30 sign-ups |
| 18 | Session 1 PILOT to 8-10 students; collect pre-quiz | How: Run Session 1 as pilot with 8-10 friendly peers. Collect 10-Q pre-quiz (needs vs wants, real vs nominal, compound, debt, budgeting). Take attendance, collect feedback form (3 Qs: what learned, confused, change). | Suggestion: Pilot with friendly peers — safe to fail. Pre-quiz is gold — without it, you can't prove lift. Feedback form that evening changes Session 2 |
| 19 | Revise from pilot; write session 2 deck | How: Morning: read feedback, adjust plan (e.g., if "too much talking," cut 5 min lecture, add 5 min game). Afternoon: write Session 2 deck with research chart integrated. | Suggestion: Start Session 2 with "Yesterday you said __, so today we will __" — shows you listened, builds trust |
| 20 | Set up Instagram/TikTok handle; brand kit in Canva (3 templates reused for all 10 posts) | How: Handle: @paraokuryazarligi or similar. Brand kit: logo (simple), 3 templates (myth-busting, recap, quiz teaser) reused for all 10 posts — don't design 10 unique. | Suggestion: 3 templates reused > 10 unique designs — saves 5 hours. One logo, two fonts, three colors max |
| 21 | Posts 1-3 published (inflation myth-busting, recap, quiz teaser) | How: Post 1: myth "Inflation = prices rise equally" (false — show your chart). Post 2: Session 1 recap with photo. Post 3: quiz teaser "What's real vs nominal?" | Suggestion: Schedule posts evening (18:00-20:00) — when students scroll. Caption in Turkish, 1-2 hashtags, question to drive comments |
| 22 | Weekly review: attendance tracker live; confirm next-week schedule | How: Count unique attendees so far (target 12 by now, 20+ by end). Check schedule: Sessions 2-5 dates, venue, projector. Confirm volunteer if any. | Suggestion: Weekly review is where 50 points come from — not from delivering, but from noticing attendance is 14 not 20 and adding personal invites |
| 23 | Session 2 (inflation) using Project 1 charts | How: Run Session 2 with her research chart as teaching material. Show headline vs food inflation gap, ask "which did you feel more?" | Suggestion: Use own chart — students see research is not abstract, teacher sees integration |
| 24 | Posts 4-6 published; reply to comments | How: Posts 4-6: debt trap case study, saving demo, installment culture. Reply to every comment within 12h. | Suggestion: Reply to every comment — algorithm rewards it, and Commenters become attendees |
| 25 | Session 3 (saving/compounding with Colab demo) | How: Live Colab demo: 100 lira/month at different rates. Students draft savings plan for one goal (phone, course). | Suggestion: Live demo > slides — one interactive chart beats five static. Have backup screenshot if WiFi fails |
| 26 | Posts 7-8; mid-series reflection note | How: Posts 7-8: earning basics teaser, mid-series reflection ("3 things we learned"). Write 1-page reflection note for portfolio. | Suggestion: Reflection note is portfolio evidence — write it while feelings fresh, not Day 30 |
| 27 | Session 4 (debt case studies) | How: Present 2 case studies, debate in pairs, then share. End with one-sentence takeaway per student. | Suggestion: Case studies with Turkish installment culture — relevant, not generic. Pair debate > whole-group lecture for engagement |
| 28 | Post 9; post-quiz logistics ready | How: Post 9: earning basics. Prepare post-quiz (same 10 Q as pre-quiz) + print. Prepare photo consent forms. | Suggestion: Same quiz as pre-quiz — not new quiz — so lift is comparable |
| 29 | Session 5 (earning); final post-quiz collected | How: Run Session 5, collect post-quiz immediately after (don't let students leave first). Final group photo with certificates (handwritten). | Suggestion: Collect post-quiz before certificates — otherwise they leave. Certificates handwritten with name > printed generic |
| 30 | IMPACT REPORT: attendance totals, pre/post lift %, quotes, photos; post 10 published | How: Report: 1 page numbers (unique attendees, average per session, pre/post mean lift %), 2 quotes (ask "what will you tell family?"), 3 photos (workshop, chart, group). Post 10: celebration recap. | Suggestion: Report page for portfolio: 3 numbers + 2 quotes + 3 photos = one page proving leadership without essay. Ask quotes before they leave, not later |

## Metrics to Capture (optimization: what counts)

- Unique attendees (target 30+) and average per session
- Pre/post quiz mean lift (target +30% relative) — this turns activity into evidence
- Two anonymized participant quotes
- Social reach screenshot pack

- [ ] 10 posts live, links archived
