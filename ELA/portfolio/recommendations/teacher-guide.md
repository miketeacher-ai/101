## Why Day 1 Is Non-Negotiable

- Strong letters need 4+ weeks and specific anecdotes; rushed letters read generic.
- Common App recommender flow has learning curve for Turkish school staff.
- ED/EA Nov 1 means letters due late October — inside busiest exam weeks.

## The Ask (in person, then email recap)

1. Ask directly: "Would you write a strong letter for my US applications?"
2. Watch for hesitation — lukewarm yes is no; have backup ready.
3. Hand over packet same day.
