## Top 10 Grammar Rules (quick list)

1. Subject-verb agreement: strip prepositional phrases.
2. Pronoun must match antecedent in number.
3. Keep verb tense consistent unless time shifts.
4. Lists must be parallel.
5. Modifiers next to what they describe.
6. Semicolon joins two complete sentences; comma cannot.
7. Colon follows complete sentence, then explains/lists.
8. Em dash sets off interruption/emphasis.
9. Apostrophe: possessive before s singular (girl's), after plural (girls').
10. Shortest correct answer often wins.
