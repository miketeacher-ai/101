## Day-by-Day: How to Build + Suggestion

### Days 16-22: Permissions + Shadowing Start — Logistics First

| Day | Task | How to Build | Suggestion / Optimization |
|-----|------|--------------|---------------------------|

Deliver in person with student ID + one-page project overview. Ask muhtar for announcement help. | Suggestion: Permission is logistics, not signature — ask muhtar how he announces (mosque loudspeaker, WhatsApp group, poster) and offer to write announcement for him. Ask poliklinik chief which days are least busy for shadowing |
| 17 | Recruit nursing-student volunteers (2-3); prepare BP/BMI station kits + education booth materials | How: Contact local vocational school nursing program. Recruit 2-3 volunteers: explain 4-hour commitment, certificate, experience. Prepare kits: BP monitor + stethoscope, scale + measuring tape, education handouts (hypertension, BMI, hygiene) in plain Turkish, pre/post quiz (10 Q). | Suggestion: Nursing students know cuffs better than you — delegate stations to them, you manage flow + education. Handouts in plain Turkish, not medical jargon — test on grandmother first |
| 18 | Shadowing block 1 (4h); log sheet: date, department, supervisor initials, learning | How: Log sheet columns: Date, Hours, Department, Supervisor Initials, One Observation (not assumption). E.g., "Nurse knew empty cabinet before opening — how?" Not "Nurse was efficient." | Suggestion: Log observations, not assumptions — "Nurse knew empty cabinet" is observation; "Nurse was efficient" is judgment. Observations make reflection note better |
| 19 | Shadowing block 2 (4h); draft health fair flyer in Canva | How: Flyer: title "Do you know your numbers? Free 5-min check + tea," date, time, venue, 3 benefits (know BP, know BMI, learn what numbers mean). A3, hand-drawn or Canva, Turkish. | Suggestion: Flyer title "Do you know your numbers?" beats "Free Health Screening" — curiosity > charity. Hand-drawn flyer signals "we care" better than printed. Test flyer on 2 neighbors — do they understand in 5 sec? |
| 20 | Shadowing block 3 (4h); finalize flyer + distribute via school/muhtar | How: Finalize flyer, print 20 copies, post at school, clinic, muhtar office, 2 shops. Ask muhtar to announce via his channel (WhatsApp/mosque). | Suggestion: Distribute via muhtar's channel — his announcement reaches 100+ faster than your 20 posters. Ask him to announce 2 days before + morning of fair |
| 21 | Shadowing block 4 (4h); prepare pre/post health-knowledge quiz (10 Q) | How: Quiz: 10 Q on hypertension, BMI, hygiene (e.g., "What BP is high? A) 120/80 B) 140/90 C) 180/110" — answer B). Same quiz pre and post for comparable lift. Print 120 copies. | Suggestion: Same quiz pre/post — not new quiz — so lift is comparable. Keep quiz to 10 Q, 5 min — longer quiz reduces participation |
| 22 | Weekly review: hours logged (16h), fair logistics checklist, confirm volunteer schedule | How: Count hours logged so far (target 16h by now, 20h by Day 23). Checklist: venue confirmed, volunteers confirmed, kits ready, flyers posted, quizzes printed, consent forms ready. Confirm volunteer arrival time for fair. | Suggestion: Weekly review is where 50 points come from — noticing volunteers not yet confirmed and confirming now, not Day 28. Checklist prevents Day 29 panic |

### Days 23-30: Shadowing Finish + Fair Execution — Proof Over Vanity

| Day | Task | How to Build | Suggestion |
|-----|------|--------------|------------|
| 23 | Shadowing block 5 (4h) — total 20h complete; request supervisor letter | How: Complete final 4h, total 20h. Ask supervisor for 1-paragraph letter: what, when, how many hours, your role (observer only), one characteristic. Provide bullet list to make it easy. | Suggestion: Provide bullet list to supervisor — makes letter specific and fast. Thank supervisor same day with handwritten note — remembered longer than email |
| 24 | Fair setup rehearsal: station layout, consent forms, photo plan | How: Visit venue, tape station layout on floor (BP table, BMI scale, 2 education booths, quiz table, tea). Prepare consent forms (Turkish + English, name + signature + "I agree to photo"). Plan photo angles (before/after same spot). | Suggestion: Tape layout on floor — reveals flow problems before fair day. Consent forms ready before fair, not during — signing during fair slows flow |
| 25 | Health fair prep: print quizzes, prepare education handouts | How: Print 120 quizzes (60 pre, 60 post + extras), 100 handouts (1 per attendee, simple: what is BP, what is BMI, when to see doctor, in plain Turkish with one illustration). Prepare quiz scoring sheet. | Suggestion: Handouts plain Turkish + one illustration > medical text. One handout per attendee, not per family — everyone leaves with something |
| 26 | Health fair prep: confirm muhtar announcement, test BP monitors | How: Confirm muhtar will announce tomorrow (Day 27) and morning of fair (Day 29). Test BP monitors on 2 people, calibrate scale. Charge phone for photos. | Suggestion: Test monitors on 2 people — discovers low battery or wrong cuff size before 100 people arrive. Confirm announcement twice — people forget |
| 27 | Shadowing reflection note (1 page) — what 20 hours taught | How: One-page reflection: what you observed (not assumed) about one doctor's limits and code's potential. Structure: 1 paragraph shadowing insight, 1 paragraph fair motivation, 1 paragraph what you learned about systems. | Suggestion: Reflection note is where shadowing becomes essay material — write while memory fresh, not Day 40. One page, not five — admissions reads concise |
| 28 | Supervisor letter follow-up | How: Gentle follow-up if letter not yet received. Offer to pick up in person. Continue fair prep: count handouts, quizzes, kits. | Suggestion: Follow up in person, not WhatsApp — shows respect. Bring thank-you again |
| 29 | FAIR DAY: BP/BMI stations + education booths for 100+ locals; metrics + photos with consent | How: Schedule: 30 min setup, 4 hours fair (10:00-14:00 avoids heat), 30 min teardown. Flow: welcome → pre-quiz (2 min) → BP/BMI stations (5 min) → education booths (5 min) → post-quiz (2 min) → tea. Photos: before/after same angle, process, group with consent. Count attendees with tally clicker. | Suggestion: Flow 2 min + 5 min + 5 min + 2 min = 14 min per person → 4 stations parallel → 100 people in 4 hours is feasible. Tea at end keeps people for post-quiz — don't serve tea first or they leave |
| 30 | IMPACT REPORT: hours logged, fair attendance, BP/BMI counts, quiz lift, quotes, photos; checkpoint done | How: Report: 1 page numbers (hours 20, attendees 127, BP measurements 127, BMI 127, education booth visits ~80, pre/post mean lift 25%), 2 quotes (ask "what will you tell family?" for better answers), 3 photos (setup, station, group). | Suggestion: Report page for portfolio: 3 numbers + 2 quotes + 3 photos = one page proving leadership without essay. Ask quotes before they leave, not later — "What will you tell family?" gets better answers than "Did you like it?" |

## Metrics to Capture (optimization: what counts for admissions)

- Shadowing hours (target 20) with supervisor letter — verifiable, not claimed
- Fair: unique attendees (target 100+, count with tally clicker), BP/BMI measurements, education booth visits
- Pre/post quiz mean lift (target +25% relative) — turns activity into evidence
- Two anonymized participant quotes — human proof

## Safety + Consent (non-negotiable, optimized)

- No clinical tasks beyond observation — observer only, documented in permission letter
- Photo consent forms for all participants (Turkish + English, name + signature + "I agree to photo")
- Adult supervisor + municipality contact on standby for entire fair
- BP monitors: not diagnostic — handout says "See doctor for interpretation" in bold

## Definition of Done (Day 30)

- [ ] 20h logged + supervisor letter requested (not just hoped for)
- [ ] Fair executed 100+ locals, metrics captured with tally and quizzes
- [ ] Reflection note (1 page) + impact report (1 page) written, photos with consent saved
