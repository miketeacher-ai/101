# Top 20 Grammar Rules & Rhetoric Patterns

**Student:** Elfin | **Major:** Molecular Biology & Medicine | **Sprint:** Day 1 Mon Aug 24 - Day 45 Wed Oct 7, 2026

Each rule: name, rule, WRONG, RIGHT, trap.

## 1. Subject-verb agreement
Rule: find true subject (ignore prepositional phrases).
Wrong: The collection of letters were donated.
Right: The collection of letters was donated.
Trap: intervening plurals disguise singular subject.

## 2. Pronoun agreement
Rule: pronoun matches antecedent in number.
Wrong: Each student must bring their laptop.
Right: Each student must bring his or her laptop.
Trap: each/every/either are singular.

## 3. Pronoun clarity
Rule: antecedent must be unambiguous.
Wrong: When Ayşe met Deniz, she had just arrived. (who?)
Right: Rewrite naming the person.
Trap: choices adding nouns are often correct.

## 4. Verb tense consistency
Rule: stay in one tense unless time shifts.
Wrong: She walked in and says hello.
Right: She walked in and said hello.
Trap: perfect tenses need anchors.

## 5. Parallelism
Rule: list items share form.
Wrong: hiking, to swim, and a bike ride
Right: hiking, swimming, and biking
Trap: watch repeated articles/prepositions.

## 6. Misplaced / dangling modifiers
Rule: modifier touches what it modifies.
Wrong: Running to class, the bell rang.
Right: Running to class, she heard the bell ring.
Trap: opening -ing must describe subject after comma.

## 7. Comma rules
Rule: commas separate list items, intro elements, nonessential — never splice sentences.
Wrong: Istanbul is huge, it never sleeps.
Right: Istanbul is huge; it never sleeps.
Trap: essential clauses take NO commas.

## 8. Semicolons
Rule: join two independent clauses or complex list items.
Wrong: She likes tea; with honey.
Right: She likes tea with honey.
Trap: both sides of ; must be complete.

## 9. Colons
Rule: colon follows COMPLETE sentence, introduces explanation/list.
Wrong: Her priorities are: family and school.
Right: She has two priorities: family and school.
Trap: part BEFORE colon must be complete.

## 10. Em dashes
Rule: dashes set off interruption/emphasis like paired commas.
Wrong: The winner - a freshman won.
Right: The winner - a freshman - shocked everyone.
Trap: paired dashes need closing dash.

## 11. Apostrophes
Rule: possessive = 's (singular) or s' (plural); contractions replace letters.
Wrong: The' students' idea's were clear.
Right: The students' ideas were clear.
Trap: its = possessive; it's = it is.

## 12. Sentence boundaries
Rule: run-ons, fragments, comma splices all break clause rules.
Wrong: Although she studied hard. She passed.
Right: Although she studied hard, she barely passed.
Trap: although/because/while make clauses dependent.

## 13. Transitions
Rule: transition must equal logic (contrast however; cause therefore; addition moreover).
Wrong: Costs rose; however, profits doubled. (same direction!)
Right: Costs rose; therefore, profits fell.
Trap: read BOTH sentences before choosing.

## 14. Redundancy / concision
Rule: shortest grammatical answer keeping meaning wins.
Wrong: reverted back, end result, past history
Right: reverted, result, history
Trap: SAT rewards deletion options often.

## 15. Logical comparison
Rule: compare like with like.
Wrong: Istanbul's weather is better than Ankara.
Right: Istanbul's weather is better than Ankara's.
Trap: comparisons hiding in "than" clauses.

## 16. Diction / precision
Rule: word must match register and exact meaning.
Wrong: The scientist was affected to the data.
Right: The scientist reacted to the data.
Trap: affect (verb) vs effect (noun); imply/infer, fewer/less.

## 17. Possessive determiners
Rule: its/their/his/her before nouns; no apostrophe ever.
Wrong: The team celebrated it's win.
Right: The team celebrated their win.
Trap: whose vs who's; their vs there.

## 18. Relative pronouns
Rule: who/whom for people, which for nonessential, that for essential.
Wrong: The scientist whom discovered it...
Right: The scientist who discovered it...
Trap: whom only after preposition or as object.

## 19. Rhetorical synthesis (bullet notes)
Rule: answer must satisfy EVERY requirement using ONLY notes.
Trap: correct-sounding choices adding facts not in notes, or dropping required detail.

## 20. Organization / expression of ideas
Rule: sentence placement follows logic: pronouns after subjects; transitions open new directions.
Trap: read surrounding sentences for where sentence fits.
