## Daily Blocks

| Block | Time | Content |
|-------|------|---------|
| AM | 70 min | Primary skill drill (from practice-schedule rotation) |
| PM | 50 min | Secondary skill or vocab/error-log |
| Micro | 15 min | Log every error with cause+fix - this is where points come from |

## Optimization Rules

1. Never skip error log. One hour of blind practice < 15 min of classified errors.
2. All mocks morning, timed, no pauses. Afternoon = review + log.
3. Writing: one human feedback/week (teacher/peer via band descriptors) > daily quantity.
4. If mock trajectory stalls 3 days, cut weakest skill's blind practice and add 20 min of targeted drill.
