# IELTS + SAT Curriculum & Resource Toolkit

## The Daily Loop (both exams)
1. **AM (70 min):** technique lesson from the relevant `lesson-plans.md`
2. **PM (50 min):** timed application set from `practice-and-mocks.md`
3. **Micro (15 min):** `apps/error-log.html` — counts, error tags, reflection, one fix
4. **10 min:** `apps/vocab-trainer.html` — 10 new + 20 review words (level-gated)

## Progressive Difficulty Ladder
Quality and difficulty **must rise** as the sprint advances. Every drill, mock, vocabulary tier, and app carries a level tag:

| Level | Days | Weeks | Material difficulty | Timing pressure | Pass standard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L1 Foundation** | 1–7 | W1 | Baseline sets, easy–medium questions, standard audio | Generous — accuracy before speed | 60–70% |
| **L2 Core** | 8–21 | W2–3 | Medium questions, mixed sets, distractors introduced | Exam time caps enforced | 75–80% |
| **L3 Integration** | 22–35 | W4–5 | Medium–hard questions, hardest passages/topics, trap-dense | Exam caps + strict flag limits | 80–85% |
| **L4 Exam** | 36–45 | W6 | Hardest official sets, 1.1× audio, shortened breaks | Exam-strict + over-speed | 85–90% under pressure |

Rules: (1) vocabulary unlocks by level — Tier 1 from Day 1, Tier 2 from Day 8, Tier 3 from Day 22 (the trainer gates this automatically); (2) mocks escalate — Mock 1/PT1 at standard conditions, the final mock/PT over-speed and exam-strict; (3) if a set is passed at its level, the next set at that level goes up one difficulty notch, never sideways; (4) the error log records the set level — still logging L1 sets in Week 3 means you are behind the ladder.

## Non-negotiables
- Every session ends in the error log — no entry, no credit.
- Every miss gets a taxonomy tag and a scheduled fix.
- Mocks are morning-only, same-day scored, same-day logged.
- Vocabulary is learned only when used in a correct collocation.
