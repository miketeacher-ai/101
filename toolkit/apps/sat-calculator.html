Desmos power moves (the exam calculator)

  • Slider-solve: type the equation, tap the slider for the unknown, read the answer. Great for messy algebra.
  • System intersection: plot both equations, tap the intersection — exact coordinates, zero arithmetic.
  • Quadratics: read roots (x-axis crossings) and vertex directly; compare forms a(x-h)²+k.
  • Regression: enter a table, then y₁ ~ a x + b — slope/intercept in seconds for line-of-best-fit questions.
  • Stats: mean(list), median(list), stdev(list) on a defined list.
  • Verify algebra: plot left side and right side as two functions — intersections are the solution set.
  • Mean-algebra check: changing one value by d moves the mean by d/n — verify with a quick list.
