# Examiner's Band 7–7.5 Guide — From 5/6 to 7

How examiners actually award bands, what separates 5/6 scripts from 7/7.5 scripts, and the upgrade drills that close the gap. Criteria below follow the public band descriptors (Writing TA/CC/LR/GRA; Speaking FC/LR/GRA/P).

## Writing Task 2 — Criterion by Criterion

| Criterion | Band 5–6 signature | Band 7–7.5 signature | Upgrade move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task Response | Position unclear or drifts; ideas listed, under-developed; 250 words padded | Position clear *throughout*; each idea extended (explain → example → implication); relevant throughout | Write ONE idea per body paragraph; test: "Can I say 3 sentences about it without repeating?" |
| Coherence & Cohesion | Mechanical linkers (Firstly, Moreover, In conclusion); referencing missing | Varied linkers, referencing (this/such + noun), clear central topic per paragraph | Delete half your linkers; replace with "this + noun" references |
| Lexical Resource | Repeats question words; general words (good, bad, things); occasional awkward phrase | Precise topic collocations; some less-common items used naturally; occasional slips only | Ban-list: good/bad/thing/very. Replace with viable, detrimental, barrier, substantially |
| Grammatical Range & Accuracy | Complex sentences attempted but frequent errors; or safe simple sentences only | Variety (relative, conditional, concessive) with errors that never block meaning | Add ONE concessive ("Although…, …") and ONE conditional per essay — no more |

## Sentence Surgery Drill (do 10 per week, log results)
Band 5: `Education is very important for students and it can help them good in future.`
Band 6: `Education plays an important role in helping students succeed in the future.`
Band 7: `A well-funded education system does more than raise grades — it equips students with the adaptability that tomorrow's jobs will demand.`

Surgery steps: (1) kill vague words → (2) add a precise collocation → (3) add a subordinate clause → (4) check it still sounds like you.

## Speaking — Criterion by Criterion

| Criterion | Band 5–6 signature | Band 7–7.5 signature | Upgrade move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fluency & Coherence | Slow, restarts, simple connectors | Speaks at length effortlessly; well-aimed discourse markers; self-correction is smooth | Never stop mid-sentence — bridge with "well,", "actually,", "what I mean is" |
| Lexical Resource | Talks about topics using basic words; sometimes wrong word | Flexibly uses idiomatic language & less common items; paraphrases on the fly | Learn 5 Tier-3 collocations per topic; paraphrase drill: explain "commuting" without the word |
| Grammatical Range & Accuracy | Short sentences, frequent slips OR over-simple | Mix of structures; slips exist but never block understanding | Rehearse ONE "If I were…, I would…" and ONE cleft ("What matters most is…") until automatic |
| Pronunciation | Mispronunciations force effort; flat delivery | Chunking, stress, intonation guide the listener; accent is irrelevant | Read one answer aloud daily marking stress; record → playback at 1.2× |

## Listening / Reading — the 6.0 → 7.0 wall
- 6.0 = 23–26 correct; 7.0 = 30–32. The missing 5–7 answers are almost always: paraphrase misses, plurals/spelling, and distractor traps — *technique errors, not knowledge errors*.
- Drill: for every miss, write the paraphrase pair you missed (`setback = problem`). Ten of these per week is the fastest 0.5 band you will ever buy.

## Self-Check Rubric (score your own essay in 5 min)
1. Is my position visible in EVERY paragraph? (TA)
2. Underline linkers — any paragraph with 4+ mechanical ones? (CC)
3. Circle repeated words — 3+ repeats of one content word? (LR)
4. Count sentences starting with "It is…" or "There is…" — more than 2? (GRA variety)
5. Any sentence you could not say out loud naturally? Rewrite it.

## Calibration Table (what to aim for per mock)

| Skill | Mock 1 | Mock 3 | Mock 5 | Exam target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | 5.0–5.5 | 6.0 | 6.5+ | 6.5–7.0 |
| Reading | 5.0–5.5 | 6.0 | 6.5+ | 6.5–7.0 |
| Writing | 5.0 | 5.5–6.0 | 6.0–6.5 | 6.0–6.5 (7.0 stretch) |
| Speaking | 5.5 | 6.0 | 6.5 | 6.5 (7.0 stretch) |

Overall 6.5 = combination (e.g., L7 R7 W6 S6). Overall 7.0 typically needs L/R at 7.5+ with W/S at 6.5 — plan your effort where points are cheapest: Listening and Reading first.
