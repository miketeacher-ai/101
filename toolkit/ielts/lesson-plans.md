# IELTS Academic Lesson Plans — 45-Day Sprint Curriculum

**Sprint window:** Day 1 Mon Aug 24 → Day 45 Wed Oct 7, 2026 · **Exam:** Day 39 Thu Oct 1 (computer-delivered)
**Daily slot:** AM 70 min primary skill · PM 50 min secondary skill · Micro 15 min error log
**Target ladder:** Band 5.0–6.5 minimum → 7.0–7.5 stretch. Every lesson below is written against official band 7+ examiner criteria.

## Phase Map (mirrors the sprint phases)

| Phase | Days | Curriculum Goal | Band Checkpoint |
|-------|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Foundation | 1–7 | Diagnose, learn question types, build daily loop | Baseline + Mock 1 |
| Core | 8–21 | Technique per question type; writing templates; fluency | Mock 2–3: L/R at 6.0+ |
| Integration | 22–35 | Weakness surgery, band-7 writing upgrades, stamina | Mock 4–5: W/S at 6.0+, L/R 6.5–7.0 |
| Polish | 36–45 | Exam simulation, error-log-only review, taper | Exam Day 39; post-exam SAT support |

## Difficulty Ladder (materials escalate every week)
| Week | Level | What gets harder |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | **L1** | Baseline Cambridge sets, S1/S2 focus, generous timing — accuracy first |
| W2–W3 | **L2** | Full question-type mix, S3/S4 enter, exam time caps enforced, distractor traps taught |
| W4–W5 | **L3** | Hardest Academic passages, trap-dense sets, 1.1× audio drills, tighter writing caps (Task 2 in 35 min) |
| W6 | **L4** | Exam-strict conditions, full over-speed simulation, zero new material — execution under pressure |

Pass standards rise with level: 60–70% (L1) → 75–80% (L2) → 80–85% (L3) → 85–90% under pressure (L4). A drill passed at its level moves UP a notch, never sideways.

## Weekly Lesson Plans

### Week 1 (Days 1–7) — Foundation: Know the Test · **L1**
- **Lesson 1.1 (Day 1) Diagnostic.** Full Cambridge test, exam conditions. Output: baseline band per skill + top-3 weakest question types written into the error log. *How:* no pausing, morning slot. *Suggestion:* observe, don't fix.
- **Lesson 1.2 (Day 2) Listening S1/S2 + prediction.** Read questions before audio; predict answer type (name/number/noun). Drill: 2 sections, prediction-first. *Time:* 2h.
- **Lesson 1.3 (Day 3) Writing Task 1 anatomy.** Overview sentence = the single highest-value sentence. Study 3 band-7 samples; copy their overview patterns. Write 1 Task 1 (20 min).
- **Lesson 1.4 (Day 4) Speaking Part 1 + Listening S3 attitude.** Record 6 answers, playback at 1.2×. Hesitation audit.
- **Lesson 1.5 (Day 5) Reading T/F/NG + Matching Headings.** T/F/NG: False = contradicts, NG = not stated. Headings = topic sentences only.
- **Lesson 1.6 (Day 6) Writing Task 2 skeleton.** 4-paragraph template; one idea per body paragraph, developed with example.
- **Lesson 1.7 (Day 7) MOCK 1** + full error classification (see practice-and-mocks.md).

### Week 2 (Days 8–14) — Core: Question-Type Technique · **L2** — time caps now enforced; Tier 2 vocabulary unlocks (Day 8)
- **Lesson 2.1 Listening S4 lectures:** abbreviation note-taking (see shared/note-taking-frameworks.md), 5/10 → 7/10 accuracy goal.
- **Lesson 2.2 Reading Multiple Choice:** eliminate 2 distractors first; correct answer = paraphrase, never word-match.
- **Lesson 2.3 Task 1 mixed charts:** overview covers ALL visuals; group data, don't list.
- **Lesson 2.4 Speaking Part 2:** 2-minute talk with story beats (what/where/why/feel); reuse personal projects as content.
- **Lesson 2.5 Listening maps/plans:** trace the speaker's path; pre-label compass and entrances.
- **Lesson 2.6 Summary completion:** word bank reveals grammar — predict word class before listening/reading.
- **Lesson 2.7 MOCK 2** + weekly rollup: shift 30% of next week's time to 2 weakest skills.

### Week 3 (Days 15–21) — Core: Production Quality · **L2+** — mixed sets at exam pace; writing under 40-min hard cap
- **Lesson 3.1 Task 1 process/map language:** passive voice, sequencers (initially, subsequently, ultimately).
- **Lesson 3.2 Speaking fluency drill:** 8 Part 1 questions, 20 s prep, 45 s answers, no stopping. Fluency before accuracy at this stage.
- **Lesson 3.3 T/F/NG advanced set** + Task 2 intro/conclusion formulas.
- **Lesson 3.4 Mixed listening mini-test (20 Q)** + Speaking Part 3 discussion depth (opinion → reason → example → concession).
- **Lesson 3.5 Task 2 cohesion for 7.0:** varied linkers (however, therefore, for instance), one linker per 2 sentences max; referencing (this/these + noun).
- **Lesson 3.6 Full Reading test, 20/20/20 pacing.**
- **Lesson 3.7 MOCK 3** + trajectory update: L/R should read 6.0; W/S typically lag — that's the Week 4–5 job.

### Week 4 (Days 22–28) — Integration: Weakness Surgery · **L3** — hardest sets only; Tier 3 vocabulary unlocks (Day 22); 1.1× audio begins
- **Lesson 4.1 Untimed→timed redo** of Mock 3's weakest listening section (isolates knowledge vs speed).
- **Lesson 4.2 Task 2 argument depth:** one idea = one paragraph + specific example; examiner wants development, not listing.
- **Lesson 4.3 Reading speed:** skimming 2 min/passage + scan keywords; inference questions last.
- **Lesson 4.4 Speaking Part 3 abstract topics:** society/technology/education frames from vocabulary.md Tier 3.
- **Lesson 4.5 Task 1 precision:** compare (whereas/while), superlatives, approximation (just under, roughly).
- **Lesson 4.6 Listening distractor traps:** corrections ("actually...", "sorry, I meant..."), negatives.
- **Lesson 4.7 MOCK 4.**

### Week 5 (Days 29–35) — Integration: Band-7 Upgrades · **L3+** — trap-dense materials, Task 2 in 35 min, full-hour writing simulation
- **Lesson 5.1 Sentence surgery** (examiner-band-7-guide.md): rewrite 10 of your own band-5/6 sentences to band 7.
- **Lesson 5.2 Task 2 position clarity:** opinion stated in intro, maintained, restated in conclusion.
- **Lesson 5.3 Speaking pronunciation for 7:** chunking, sentence stress, intonation on opinion words.
- **Lesson 5.4 Reading hardest sets** (Academic passages: science/History) — inference + writer's view questions.
- **Lesson 5.5 Task 1 + Task 2 full hour simulation** (20 + 40 min, no pause).
- **Lesson 5.6 Listening full test at 1.1× speed** (exam feels slower afterward).
- **Lesson 5.7 MOCK 5** — dress rehearsal with computer-delivered interface practice.

### Week 6 (Days 36–45) — Polish & Exam · **L4** — exam-strict only; over-speed rehearsal; no new material
- **Days 36–38:** error-log-only review; re-do every logged miss cold; light speaking warm-ups; NO new material.
- **Day 39 (Thu Oct 1): IELTS EXAM.** Morning exam protocol in practice-and-mocks.md.
- **Days 40–45:** SAT priority (see sat/lesson-plans.md); one IELTS maintenance set per 2 days only if needed.

## Daily Loop (non-negotiable)
1. AM technique lesson (70 min) → 2. PM application set (50 min) → 3. Micro: error log every miss with taxonomy + one fix (15 min) → 4. 10 min vocabulary tier work in apps/vocab-trainer.html.
