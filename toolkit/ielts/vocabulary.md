# IELTS Vocabulary Mastery — Tiered by Band, Grouped by Theme

Load these into `apps/vocab-trainer.html` (filter: IELTS). Rule: a word is *learned* when you can use it in a correct collocation, not when you recognize it. 10 new + 20 review words per day, 10 minutes.

## How examiners read vocabulary
- **Band 5:** enough for familiar topics; word-choice errors; repeats question words.
- **Band 6:** adequate range, some precision, occasional awkward collocation.
- **Band 7:** flexibility + precision: less-common items, natural collocations, occasional slips only.
- **Band 7.5+:** idiomatic and topic-fluent; near-native precision in your strong topics.

## Tier 1 — Band 5.0–6.0 Core (must be automatic)
**Change/trend:** increase, decrease, rise, fall, grow, drop, remain stable
**Opinion:** think, believe, agree, important, useful, necessary
**Society:** city, community, government, law, citizen, public
**Study:** research, study, knowledge, skill, method, result
**Verbs of impact:** help, improve, reduce, cause, affect, provide
*Self-test:* use each in a sentence about your own life. Hesitation = not learned.

### Task 1 Chart Language (Academic)
**Verbs:** surge, climb, decline, plummet, plateau, fluctuate, double, triple
**Adverbs:** steadily, sharply, gradually, marginally, dramatically
**Nouns:** an upward trend, a threefold increase, a slight dip, a peak, a plateau
**Comparing:** whereas, while, by contrast, the reverse was true for, respectively, just under/over, roughly a quarter of
*Overview template:* "Overall, [A] rose steadily across the period, whereas [B] declined marginally, leaving [A] as the dominant category by 2024."

### Discourse & Cohesion Kit
concession: admittedly, granted, while it is true that
result: consequently, as a result, hence
example: for instance, notably, take… as a case in point
contrast: conversely, on the contrary, whereas
conclusion: ultimately, on balance, all things considered
*Rule:* one linker per ~2 sentences; pair each with referencing ("this measure", "such a shift").
