# SAT Lesson Plans — 45-Day Sprint Curriculum (Digital SAT)

**Sprint window:** Day 1 Mon Aug 24 → Day 45 Wed Oct 7 · **Real SAT:** Day 41 Sat Oct 3 · **PTs:** Days 12, 22, 32, 39
**Format:** Digital adaptive SAT — R&W 2×32-min modules (54 Q), Math 2×35-min modules (44 Q), Desmos calculator allowed throughout Math.
**Weekly rhythm (mirrors the sprint):** Mon/Wed/Fri Math · Tue/Thu R&W · Sat mixed set · Sun test or full review.

## Phase Map

| Phase | Days | Goal | Score checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 1–7 | Diagnostic, Bluebook familiarity, Desmos fluency | Baseline |
| Core | 8–21 | Domain technique + timed sets | PT1 (Day 12) → PT2 (Day 22) |
| Integration | 22–35 | Adaptive-module strategy, weakness surgery | PT3 (Day 32) |
| Polish | 36–45 | Simulation + taper | PT4 (Day 39) → REAL (Day 41) |

## Difficulty Ladder (materials escalate every week)
| Week | Level | What gets harder |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | **L1** | Easy–medium Bluebook sets, Desmos-first allowed on everything, generous pacing — accuracy first |
| W2–W3 | **L2** | Medium sets at module pace (71 s/Q R&W, 90 s/Q Math), Desmos allowed but timed |
| W4–W5 | **L3** | Medium–hard sets, algebra-first on easy Qs (Desmos only for verification), hard Module-2 simulations, trap-dense |
| W6 | **L4** | Hardest official questions, exam-strict conditions, no-Desmos reflex drills, full simulations |

Pass standards rise: 60–70% (L1) → 75–80% (L2) → 80–85% (L3) → 85–90% under pressure (L4). Passed a set? The next one goes up a notch — never sideways.

## Weekly Lesson Plans

### Week 1 (Days 1–7) — Foundation · **L1** — easy–medium only, Desmos-first everywhere
- **Lesson S1.1 Diagnostic (Day 1):** Bluebook full practice test. Output: baseline per domain (Algebra / Advanced Math / PSAD / Geometry / R&W Craft-Structure / Information-Ideas).
- **Lesson S1.2 Desmos bootcamp:** solve equations by slider/intersection instead of algebra; regression for line-of-best-fit; `mean()/median()` on lists. (apps/sat-calculator.html guide section.)
- **Lesson S1.3 R&W passage economy:** read questions first for vocab-in-context; passage maps only for Evidence/Inference sets.
- **Lesson S1.4 Standard English rules audit:** score the writing-grammar drill; rank the 8 rules by personal error count.
- **Lesson S1.5 Math grid-in hygiene:** student-produced responses — fractions vs decimals, no mixed numbers, / as fraction bar.
- **Lesson S1.6 Mixed set (Sat):** 20 Q half-module, log everything.
- **Lesson S1.7 Review (Sun):** error-log rollup; pick 2 weakest domains for Week 2 weighting.

### Weeks 2–3 (Days 8–21) — Core · **L2** — medium sets at module pace; Advanced Math vocabulary unlocks (Day 8)
- **Math lessons:** linear systems (substitution vs Desmos intersection), inequalities (flip rule; number line), quadratic vertex/factored forms, function notation & transformations, percent change chains, ratios→rates→proportions, scatterplot line-of-best-fit in context, exponent rules, radicals, absolute value, circle equations.
- **R&W lessons:** command of evidence (find the line BEFORE choosing), words-in-context (predict your own word first), transitions (contrast/cause/addition taxonomy), paired passages (agree/disagree map), rhetorical synthesis (answer must satisfy ALL bullets), text purpose & structure.
- **Sat mixed sets:** 27-Q R&W module + 22-Q Math module alternate weeks.
- **Day 12 PT1, Day 14 review, Day 21 mini-review** — full protocol in practice-and-mocks.md.

### Weeks 4–5 (Days 22–35) — Integration · **L3** — hard sets, algebra-first on easy questions, Advanced vocabulary in play (Day 22)
- **Adaptive strategy:** Module 1 accuracy unlocks the harder Module 2 — the first 10 questions of Module 1 decide your ceiling. Rule: never spend 3+ min on one Module-1 question.
- **Weakness surgery:** each AM = one domain drill at 80% accuracy target untimed → then timed. PM = mixed set.
- **Desmos power moves 2:** systems with no/infinite solutions (parallel/coincident lines), quadratic roots by graph, triangle area by vertex list, mean algebra ("if the mean is k…").
- **R&W timing:** 54 Q / 64 min ≈ 71 s per question — bank time on vocab/grammar for synthesis questions.
- **Day 32 PT3** + deep review.

### Week 6 (Days 36–45) — Polish & Exam · **L4** — hardest official questions, exam-strict, no new material
- **Days 36–38:** error-log-only review; redo every logged miss cold; one Desmos speed circuit.
- **Day 39:** PT4 morning (IELTS exam is the same day — PT4 optional if exhausted; move to Day 40 morning).
- **Day 40:** light review only — formula sheet skim, sleep 8 h.
- **Day 41 (Sat Oct 3) REAL SAT:** protocol in practice-and-mocks.md.
- **Days 42–45:** portfolio sprint (see portfolio/master-plan.md).

## Daily Loop
AM technique (70) → PM timed set (50) → error log with taxonomy + one fix (15) → 10 min vocab (apps/vocab-trainer.html, SAT filter).
