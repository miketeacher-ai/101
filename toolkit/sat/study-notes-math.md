# SAT Math Study Notes — Top Percentile Playbook

Digital SAT Math: 44 questions, 68 min, two 35-min adaptive modules, ~22 Q per module, Desmos allowed on ALL questions. ~75% multiple choice, ~25% student-produced response (grid-in).

## How deep to go — by difficulty level
- **L1 (W1):** Algebra domain + Desmos-first on everything. Accuracy before speed.
- **L2 (W2–3):** all four domains at module pace; grid-in hygiene automatic; Advanced Math begins.
- **L3 (W4–5):** algebra-first on easy/medium Qs (Desmos verifies, doesn't solve), hard Module-2 sets, remainder theorem + conics depth.
- **L4 (W6):** hardest official questions, no-Desmos reflex on easy/medium, slip checklist before every submit.

## Domain Map & Priorities

| Domain | Share | Your leverage |
|---|---|---|
| Algebra (linear) | ~35% | Highest frequency, most learnable — perfect this first |
| Advanced Math (nonlinear) | ~35% | Quadratics/functions/exponents — the 700+ territory |
| Problem Solving & Data | ~15% | Ratios, percents, stats — fast points with Desmos |
| Geometry & Trig | ~15% | Formulas + special triangles — fixed question shapes |

## Algebra (the engine room)
- **Solving linear equations:** do it algebraically when simple; Desmos when messy. Check by substitution — 10 s insurance.
- **Systems:** substitution/elimination for exact values; graph both and tap intersection for speed. Infinite solutions = same line (equal slopes AND intercepts); no solution = parallel.
- **Inequalities:** flip the sign when multiplying/dividing by a negative; word problems: define the variable BEFORE writing the inequality.
- **Linear models in context:** slope = rate (units per unit), intercept = starting value. Wrong-answer traps swap these roles.

## Advanced Math (700+ material)
- **Quadratics — three forms, three uses:**
  - standard `ax²+bx+c` → y-intercept = c
  - factored `a(x-p)(x-q)` → roots p, q
  - vertex `a(x-h)²+k` → vertex (h, k); a<0 opens down
- **Desmos move:** type it, read roots/vertex off the graph; for "how many real solutions" count x-axis crossings.
- **Function notation:** f(x+h) shifts RIGHT by h; f(x)+k shifts UP. Composition: work inside-out.
- **Exponent rules:** `x^a·x^b=x^(a+b)`, `(x^a)^b=x^(ab)`, `x^(-n)=1/x^n`, `x^(1/n)=ⁿ√x`. Same-base equate-exponents is the recurring trick.
- **Exponential models:** growth `A=P(1+r)^t`, decay `A=P(1-r)^t`; interpret r as a percent per time unit.
- **Polynomials:** remainder theorem — remainder of ÷(x−k) is f(k). Factor theorem: f(k)=0 ⟺ (x−k) is a factor.

## Problem Solving & Data
- **Percent change chain:** multiply factors, never add percents: +20% then −20% = 1.2×0.8 = 0.96 (−4%, not 0).
- **Units & rates:** convert FIRST, compute SECOND. Dollars/hour vs dollars/minute is the classic trap.
- **Stats:** mean algebra — "if the mean is k and one value changes by d, the mean changes by d/n". Median: order first, always. Outliers move mean, not median.
- **Line of best fit:** slope and intercept in context; interpolation vs extrapolation language ("predicts", "not reliable outside the data").
- **Probability & two-way tables:** P(event) = favorable/total; conditional = row/column-restricted; "at least one" = 1 − P(none).

## Geometry & Trig
- **Circles:** (x−h)²+(y−k)²=r² — complete the square to find center/radius; arc = (angle/360)·2πr; sector = (angle/360)·πr².
- **Triangles:** similar ⇒ proportional sides; area = ½ab·sin C; special rights: 45-45-90 (x, x, x√2), 30-60-90 (x, x√3, 2x).
- **Trig:** SOH-CAH-TOA + complementary identity sin θ = cos(90−θ). Radians↔degrees: ×π/180.
- **Volume:** provided on the formula sheet — know when to set V₁=V₂ for conservation problems.

## Grid-In Hygiene (student-produced response)
- Fractions or decimals only — NO mixed numbers (write 5/2, not 2 1/2).
- Decimals: 4 slots — .666 or .667 both accepted; never round to .67.
- Negative answers never appear; if you get one, re-read the question.
- Reduce fractions only if needed to fit; both 6/8 and 3/4 score.

## Desmos Power Moves (apps/sat-calculator.html has the full guide)
1. **Slider-solve:** type equation, add slider for unknown, read intersection.
2. **System intersection:** plot both, tap the point — exact coordinates.
3. **Regression:** enter table, `y1 ~ a x + b` — read slope/intercept for line-of-best-fit questions.
4. **Mean/median fast check:** `mean(list)`, `median(list)`.
5. **Quadratic roots:** count x-axis crossings; read vertex directly.
6. **Verify algebra answers:** plot both sides as separate functions — intersection = solution set.
